Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a beautiful landlocked country in Southern Africa known for its rich culture, dramatic landscapes, and welcoming people. Its most iconic natural treasure is Victoria Falls on the Zambezi River, known locally as Mosi-oa-Tunya ('The Smoke That Thunders')—one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World.
